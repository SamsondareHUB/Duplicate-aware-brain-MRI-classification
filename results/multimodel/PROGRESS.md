# Resource safety stop and recovery record

At the time of the isolated-fold recovery, **4 verified completed new runs** were present, all MobileNetV2: A folds 1–2 and B folds 1–2. This is a historical checkpoint; the current count is the number of validated run JSON and prediction pairs in `results/multimodel/runs/`. Here A means patient-disjoint and B means matched image-level seed 11, following the frozen PR #1 split filenames. The newer multi-model specification reverses these letters; do not reassign existing results.

MobileNetV2 B fold 2 was initially interrupted during epoch 9 of 10 by `scripts/safe_train.py` when host swap usage rose more than 0.5 GiB above that session's baseline (4.925 to 5.636 GiB). The interrupted attempt produced **no valid prediction file or run JSON**. The monitor did not start another fold. macOS reported no thermal warning; available RAM was 5.98 GiB and memory-pressure free was 33% at the cutoff. After recovery, B fold 2 was rerun in a fresh process and **completed** in 190 seconds without further swap growth. The interrupted attempt is not counted as a completed run.

The official Figshare download script reported all six published MD5 checksums matched. The dataset manifest has 3,064 images, 233 patients, and class counts 708/1,426/930. Both frozen split files matched `data/splits/split_validation.json`. The first complete fold took 176 seconds on the Mac's MPS backend. The three completed folds took 176, 176, and 172 seconds respectively.

At that checkpoint, no MobileNetV2 model-level result or model ranking was available. The remaining work then was 6 MobileNetV2 folds, 10 EfficientNet-B0 folds, 10 DenseNet121 folds, then joint analysis, independent verification, scientific report, and PR. `scripts/serial_model.py` executes one fold per fresh process with a cooling interval and a shared swap-growth baseline across the model. It stops on any unsafe resource reading; it does not automatically retry a failed fold. `scripts/run_multimodel.py` validates existing completed fold files and skips them when it resumes.

EfficientNet-B0 completed A folds 1–3 and B folds 1–2. During B fold 3, the isolated process was stopped after epoch 3 when swap rose from 3.155 to 3.695 GiB within that run. After exit, no trainer or process-scoped `caffeinate` remained; available RAM recovered to 10.16 GiB and swap remained about 3.68 GiB.

**Completed count at the safety stop: 15 of 30 new runs** (MobileNetV2 10/10, EfficientNet-B0 5/10, DenseNet121 0/10). The 15 runs then remaining were EfficientNet-B0 B fold 3 plus A/B folds 4–5, and all 10 DenseNet121 folds. EfficientNet-B0 model-level results and four-model rankings must not be reported from partial folds. Training was paused for system safety; the existing outputs and temporary workspace were preserved for resumption after memory and swap recovered.

After recovery, B fold 3 was rerun once in a fresh monitored process with idle MPS allocator blocks released between epochs. It completed all 10 epochs with no swap growth during the retry and a minimum observed available RAM of 7.13 GiB. The interruption remains documented; the completed retry is the only B fold 3 prediction set. This was a resource-management adjustment, not a change to preprocessing, model weights, batch size, optimizer, schedule, augmentation, seeds, or evaluation protocol. At this checkpoint, 16 of 30 new runs are complete.
